Gold (Au) — Lead-Collection Fire Assay (Pb-FA)

Standard 30–50 g litharge (PbO) fusion at 1000–1100 °C followed by cupellation at ~950 °C to a doré prill. Finishes: Gravimetric microbalance (high-grade/bullion), FAAS, ICP-OES, or ICP-MS.

Benchmark Elements:

1. Lead-Collection Fire Assay (Pb-FA)

  • Elements: Au, Ag + Pt, Pd, Rh (with Au/Ag collector prill)
  • Collector & Flux: Litharge (PbO), Na2CO3, borax, silica, flour/KNO3 (30–50 g aliquot)
  • Thermal Cycle: Fusion 1000–1100 °C to Pb button; cupellation ~950 °C
  • Finish & LLDs: ICP-MS (1–2 ppb), FAAS (5–10 ppb), ICP-OES, Gravimetric (~0.05 g/t)

2. Nickel Sulfide Fire Assay (NiS-FA)

  • Elements: Full 6-PGE Suite: Ru, Rh, Pd, Os, Ir, Pt
  • Collector & Flux: Ni powder + elemental S (NiS matte button) with borax/soda flux
  • Dissolution: Fusion 1000–1150 °C; conc. HCl dissolution + Tellurium (Te) co-precipitation
  • Finish & LLDs: ICP-MS (0.5–2 ppb; Os 2–5 ppb sealed digest), ICP-OES, INAA

3. Precious Metal Units & Metric Conversions

  • Metric Equivalents: 1 ppm = 1 mg/kg = 1 g/t | 1 ppb = 0.001 ppm
  • Weight Percent: 10,000 ppm = 1 wt.% | 1 wt.% = 10,000 g/t
  • Troy Mass: 1 troy ounce = 31.1034768 g
  • Tonnage Grades: 1 oz troy/t = 31.10348 g/t | 1 oz troy/short ton = 34.2857 g/t
